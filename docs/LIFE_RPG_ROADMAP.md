# Spend It All — Optional Life / RPG Economy Roadmap

## Product rule
The Life / RPG layer deepens the economic simulation without replacing the fast Spend It All loop. It is optional. When disabled, skill gates are ignored and the original economy remains playable. When enabled, the same run gains a character background, stats, skills, learning, realistic early work, housing costs and additional progression gates.

## Implemented foundation

### Character build
- One-time starting backgrounds: Blank Slate, Reliable Grinder, People Person, Hands-On Maker, Creative Starter, Digital Native.
- Stats: Grit, Focus, People, Knowledge, Adaptability.
- Stats influence skill-learning speed; housing quality can also slightly affect learning efficiency.
- Ten skills: General Work, Hospitality, Sales, Creative, Technology, Finance, Management, Trades, Media, Real Estate.
- Skills advance from level 0 to 10 through XP.

### Learn in your own time
Free or very low-cost study activities exist for every skill and consume schedule time when simulated time is enabled. Learning is available from the Time / Schedule surface rather than buried in Settings.

### Zero-cash and low-cash work
The active earning ladder starts with microtasks, recyclables, neighborhood help, pet care and odd jobs, then grows through yard work, delivery, cleaning, temp shifts, creative/tech gigs, sales, repair, bookkeeping, media and property services before the existing high-value contract tiers.

### Earlier passive income
The passive ladder now starts with a $250 Snack Cooler and adds Print-on-Demand, Tool Rental, Local Ad Page and Storage / Resale operations before the existing vending, laundromat, parking, data center, fund and civilization-scale streams.

### Realistic starter businesses
Managed businesses now begin with small-company entry points instead of jumping straight to a $750K restaurant group:
- Solo Service Business
- Cleaning Service
- Lawn Care Business
- Mobile Detailing
- Food Cart
- Online Shop
- Bookkeeping Practice
- Creative Agency
- Tech Consultancy
- Property Services

These grow into the existing restaurant, retail, software, hotel, manufacturing, media and banking companies. In Life RPG mode, relevant skills are required. Outside Life RPG mode, those gates are ignored.

### Housing ladder
The personal housing system is separate from empire-scale House / Town progression. Current choices include:
- Stay With Someone
- Temporary Shelter
- Budget Motel
- Rent a Room
- Shared Apartment
- Studio Lease
- One-Bedroom Lease
- Small House Rental
- Starter Condo
- Starter House
- Townhome

Housing has move-in cost, recurring daily equivalent, stability and focus/recovery metadata. Owned housing contributes to net worth. Recurring housing cost follows simulated game days; Classic Time does not silently advance rent while the clock is disabled.

## Next expansion waves

### Career system
Add applications, hourly jobs, salaried jobs, promotions, schedules, layoffs and career reputation. Skills should unlock careers rather than cash spent. Candidate ladders:
- food/service → supervisor → manager → regional operations
- warehouse/logistics → lead → dispatcher → logistics management
- support/IT → technician → administrator → engineer
- design/media → junior → specialist → director → agency owner
- sales → representative → account executive → enterprise sales
- bookkeeping → accountant → controller → finance leadership
- trades → apprentice → technician → contractor
- property services → agent/manager → operator/developer

### Education and credentials
Add free learning, paid courses, certificates, apprenticeships, trade licenses and degrees as distinct paths. Expensive education should not always be the best route; experience and self-study can reach many of the same opportunities more slowly.

### Personal budget layer
Optional costs can include food, utilities, phone, transportation, insurance and subscriptions. Keep this behind Life RPG / simulation settings so the simple game is never buried under bills.

### Transportation
Walking/transit/bike/used car/new car should change access, time cost and job availability. Vehicles can introduce fuel, insurance and maintenance only when the deeper simulation is enabled.

### Housing depth
Future housing should support deposits, roommate splits, lease length, rent increases, utilities, eviction risk, mortgages, refinancing, property tax, insurance and maintenance. Mortgage debt should eventually use the existing explicit Debt & Court system rather than a second incompatible debt model.

### Networking and reputation
Add local reputation, client trust, professional network and references. These should unlock better clients, jobs and business opportunities without simply acting as another currency.

### Skill-driven economics
Use skills to alter capability more than raw multipliers: new gigs, better contract sizes, fewer business mistakes, wider deal access, improved hiring/operations and better information. Avoid turning every skill into a generic +10% income bonus.

### Life events
Examples: roommate moves out, rent increase, broken appliance, client referral, unexpected medical/repair cost, certification opportunity, job offer, layoff, local hiring boom, housing shortage, transit disruption and family/friend support. World events and personal events should interact without becoming constant punishment.

### Card Shop career scenario
A later economic scenario can begin as a Card Shop employee with an employee discount and progress through buyer, manager, reseller, store owner and collectibles chain. This remains an economic specialization; card-battle gameplay stays deferred unless explicitly approved later.

## UX rules
- Keep Life RPG optional and toggleable.
- Never hide the fast core money loop behind mandatory character management.
- Put settings in Settings; put choices and progression on gameplay surfaces.
- On mobile, show immediate earning choices before long reference information.
- Locked options must say exactly what unlocks them.
- Preserve old saves by normalizing missing Life RPG state.
- Existing LOK/Looper cosmetics remain separate from economic power.
